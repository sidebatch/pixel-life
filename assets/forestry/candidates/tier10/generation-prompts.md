# Tier 10 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references. Tier 10 is the visual endpoint of forestry progression, so every species has a different dominant feature.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left fantasy-forest light; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `origin_tree`: immense straight central trunk, layered stone-brown and jade bark, colossal roots, three balanced emerald tiers, restrained turquoise sap seams.
- `primal_ancient`: three fused bronze-gray trunks, wildly asymmetrical branches, huge irregular moss-green, ochre, and dark teal crown, broken limbs and tangled roots.
- `worldroot`: lower two-thirds dominated by interwoven earth-brown roots forming arches and cavern-like gaps, short trunk, compact deep-green crown.
- `dawncore`: near-black bark shell split by broad golden-orange inner seams, gold-lined roots, burgundy-to-sunrise-gold crown.
- `abysswood`: almost-black ridged trunk, claw-like roots, low umbrella crown of black-indigo, blue-violet, and muted plum leaves.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `origin_tree`: immense jade-brown bark, broad pale jade-tan face with dense rings, colossal roots, moss and turquoise seams.
- `primal_ancient`: irregular three-lobed fused-trunk stump, bronze-gray bark, uneven golden rings, tangled roots, ochre and teal leaves.
- `worldroot`: short stump on a dominant mound of interwoven roots and root arches, warm dense rings, moss and turquoise-tipped leaves.
- `dawncore`: near-black bark with gold seams, vivid gold-orange heartwood, gold-lined roots, dawn-colored leaf sprig.
- `abysswood`: almost-black bark, indigo-violet face with cobalt rings, claw-like roots, blue-violet and plum leaves.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; exactly two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut faces and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `origin_tree`: jade-brown layered bark, pale jade-tan faces, moss, turquoise seams, emerald leaf sprig.
- `primal_ancient`: bronze-gray irregular bark, uneven golden rings and cracks, moss, ochre and teal leaves.
- `worldroot`: thick interwoven-root bark, warm dense rings, moss and turquoise-tipped leaves.
- `dawncore`: near-black bark with gold-orange seams, vivid golden faces, dawn-colored leaves.
- `abysswood`: almost-black ridged bark, indigo-violet faces with cobalt rings, blue-violet and plum leaves.
