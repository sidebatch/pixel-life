# Tier 8 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references. Tier 8 keeps a believable ancient-forest look while introducing restrained fantasy traits.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left daylight; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `ancient_zelkova`: immense broad layered crown, huge weathered warm-gray hollowed trunk, massive exposed roots, sparse moss, restrained amber old leaves.
- `amber_cedar`: tall ancient cedar, layered dark-green boughs, reddish furrowed trunk, restrained amber resin beads and seams.
- `silverbark`: smooth silver-white bark with dark scars, elegant branching and pale roots, layered cool teal-green crown.
- `spiralwood`: pronounced continuous corkscrew trunk and roots, tawny helix bark ridges, asymmetrical olive-green crown.
- `moonshade`: charcoal-gray trunk, low spreading branches, dark navy-blue-green crown, silver-gray leaf undersides forming pale patches.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `ancient_zelkova`: massive weathered gray bark and roots, dense golden rings, hollow notch, moss and amber leaves.
- `amber_cedar`: reddish furrowed bark, honey-gold rings, amber resin beads, cedar sprig.
- `silverbark`: silver-white bark with charcoal scars, pearl-gray face and blue-gray rings, teal sprig.
- `spiralwood`: corkscrew bark ridge wrapping trunk and roots, golden face with spiral rings, olive leaf.
- `moonshade`: charcoal bark, muted violet-gray face with silver rings, dark leaves with silver undersides.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; exactly two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut faces and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `ancient_zelkova`: weathered gray bark, dense golden rings, moss and amber leaves.
- `amber_cedar`: reddish furrowed bark, honey-gold faces, amber resin beads, cedar sprig.
- `silverbark`: silver-white bark, pearl-gray faces with blue-gray rings, teal leaves.
- `spiralwood`: strong corkscrew bark ridges and visible spiral rings, olive leaf.
- `moonshade`: charcoal bark, muted violet-gray faces with silver rings, navy leaves with silver undersides.
