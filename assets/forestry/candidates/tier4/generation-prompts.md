# Tier 4 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, and scale references.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left light; readable at 120×144; centered with the trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `ash`: tall upright balanced silhouette, straight pale gray-brown trunk, airy oval canopy, compound ash leaf clusters, cool medium green.
- `teak`: sturdy warm golden-brown trunk, broad dense tropical canopy, visibly large leathery oval leaves, deep olive and warm green.
- `mahogany`: elegant spreading crown, layered dark emerald foliage, graceful branches, deep reddish-brown trunk, subtle burgundy undertones.
- `mango`: stout warm-brown trunk, low spreading branches, dense glossy narrow dark-green leaves, five to seven restrained golden-orange mangoes.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each new living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and growth rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `ash`: pale gray-brown bark, light cream cut face, fine rings, two tiny compound leaflets.
- `teak`: warm golden-brown bark, honey-tan cut face, bold rings, one small broad leaf.
- `mahogany`: deep reddish-brown bark, warm red-tan cut face, tight rings, two tiny dark-green leaflets.
- `mango`: warm orange-brown bark, golden-tan cut face, one narrow leaf and one small golden mango.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each new living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; two short cut log segments in a compact diagonal stack; visible circular cut face and growth rings; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `ash`: pale beige cut faces, gray-brown bark, straight fine grain, tiny compound leaf sprig.
- `teak`: honey-gold cut faces, golden-brown bark, strong warm grain, one broad leaf.
- `mahogany`: warm salmon cut faces, deep reddish-brown bark, dark red grain, two tiny leaflets.
- `mango`: light golden cut faces, orange-brown bark, subtle wavy grain, one narrow leaf and one small mango.
