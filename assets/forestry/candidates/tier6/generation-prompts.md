# Tier 6 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left daylight; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `olive`: low asymmetric crown, twisted hollowed silver-gray trunk, knotted roots, narrow silvery leaves, restrained dark olives.
- `purpleheart`: tall dark trunk with muted violet undertones, high tropical crown, small purple wood scar, pale lavender blossoms.
- `jatoba`: massive reddish-brown trunk, buttress roots, dense tropical crown, paired leaves, restrained copper pods.
- `spotted_gum`: tall smooth cream-gray-olive mottled trunk, high open crown, long narrow gray-green leaves.
- `ironbark`: near-black deeply furrowed trunk, heavy branches, rugged blue-green crown, restrained dusty-crimson blossoms.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `olive`: twisted silver-gray bark, hollow creases, pale irregular rings, silvery leaf and dark olives.
- `purpleheart`: charcoal bark, vivid purple-violet heartwood with darker rings and pale sapwood rim, leaf and lavender blossom.
- `jatoba`: red-brown bark, brick-red heartwood, golden sapwood rim, paired leaf and pod.
- `spotted_gum`: smooth cream-gray-olive mottled bark, honey cut face, narrow leaf sprig.
- `ironbark`: near-black deeply furrowed bark, dark red-brown dense rings, blue-green leaves and crimson blossom.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut face and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `olive`: twisted silver-gray bark, pale faces with wavy olive-toned rings, silvery leaf and dark olives.
- `purpleheart`: charcoal bark, vivid purple-violet heartwood and pale sapwood rim, leaves and lavender blossom.
- `jatoba`: red-brown bark, brick-red faces with dark rings and golden rim, leaf and pod.
- `spotted_gum`: smooth cream-gray-olive mottled bark, honey faces, narrow gray-green leaf sprig.
- `ironbark`: near-black furrowed bark, dark red-brown faces with dense rings, blue-green leaf and crimson blossom.
