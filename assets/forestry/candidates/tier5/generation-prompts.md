# Tier 5 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left daylight; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `baobab`: extremely thick bottle-shaped pale warm-gray trunk, broad roots, stout upward branches, compact muted-green leaf clusters.
- `sequoia`: monumental tall tapering silhouette, cinnamon-red deeply furrowed trunk, narrow conical crown, tiered dark blue-green boughs.
- `black_locust`: charcoal-brown furrowed bark, rugged irregular branches, airy asymmetric compound-leaf crown, three restrained creamy flower clusters.
- `hickory`: thick gray-brown trunk with shaggy peeling bark, heavy oval crown, robust branches, compound leaves, three small nut husks.
- `eucalyptus`: smooth cream-gray-tan-salmon patchwork bark, open high crown, long drooping blue-green leaves, visible branch structure.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `baobab`: extremely broad pale swollen base, rounded roots, broad cream-tan face, tiny leaf cluster.
- `sequoia`: cinnamon-red fibrous bark, strong roots, red cut face with many tight rings, tiny conifer sprig.
- `black_locust`: dark furrowed bark, irregular roots, honey cut face, leaflet and small flower cluster.
- `hickory`: gray-brown shaggy bark strips, sturdy roots, bold rings, leaf and nut husk.
- `eucalyptus`: smooth multicolor patchwork bark, golden face, narrow blue-green leaf sprig.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut face and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `baobab`: pale warm-gray bark, broad creamy faces, wide uneven rings, tiny leaf cluster.
- `sequoia`: cinnamon-red fibrous bark, red-orange faces, many tight rings, tiny conifer sprig.
- `black_locust`: charcoal-brown furrowed bark, pale golden faces, dense rings, leaflet and small flower cluster.
- `hickory`: shaggy gray-brown bark, warm beige faces, bold rings, leaf and nut husk.
- `eucalyptus`: cream-gray-salmon patchwork bark, light golden faces, narrow drooping leaf sprig.
