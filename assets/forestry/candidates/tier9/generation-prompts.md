# Tier 9 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references. Tier 9 deliberately introduces colors and silhouettes that are rare in nature while preserving the established game style.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; cool upper-left light; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `spirit_ancient`: monumental chalk-white weathered trunk, deep hollows, enormous exposed roots, strongly asymmetrical branches, pale sage and mist-blue leaves, restrained cyan spirit motes.
- `starlight_tree`: midnight indigo trunk, deep navy-violet crown, many tiny warm-white and pale-blue star points with a few brighter pixel glints.
- `moonveil`: weeping-willow silhouette, muted lavender-gray trunk, long separated curtains of silver-white leaves with pale blue tips.
- `crystal_leaf`: pale blue-gray trunk, angular branches, faceted turquoise, cyan, and sapphire crystal-leaf clusters.
- `whisperwood`: tall slender smoke-gray trunk, very thin long branches, five separated tiers of small sea-green leaves, drooping twig tips.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `spirit_ancient`: chalk-white bark, pearly ivory face with blue-gray rings, hollow notch, sage leaves and tiny cyan motes.
- `starlight_tree`: midnight indigo bark, violet-blue face with pale rings, navy leaves and restrained star points.
- `moonveil`: lavender-gray bark, silver-white face with icy blue rings, small hanging silver leaf curtain.
- `crystal_leaf`: pale blue-gray bark, cyan heartwood with angular ring bands, turquoise and sapphire crystal leaves.
- `whisperwood`: smoke-gray fine-lined bark, pale olive-gray face with delicate rings, thin roots and airy sea-green sprig.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; exactly two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut faces and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `spirit_ancient`: chalk-white bark, pearly ivory faces with blue-gray rings, sage leaves and restrained cyan motes.
- `starlight_tree`: midnight indigo bark, violet-blue faces and pale rings, navy leaves and star points.
- `moonveil`: lavender-gray bark, silver-white faces with icy blue rings, hanging silver leaves.
- `crystal_leaf`: pale blue-gray bark, cyan geometric heartwood bands, faceted turquoise and sapphire leaves.
- `whisperwood`: smoke-gray fine-lined bark, pale olive-gray faces with narrow rings, airy sea-green sprig.
