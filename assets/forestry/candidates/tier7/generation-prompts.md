# Tier 7 forestry generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen mode was used with transparent backgrounds. Existing Pixel Life sprites were supplied only as style, composition, viewpoint, pixel-density, and scale references.

## Living trees

Common prompt: create one complete mature overworld tree sprite; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down RPG view; crisp dark pixel edging; upper-left daylight; readable at 120×144; centered with trunk base at bottom center; no ground, shadow, scenery, border, text, stump, or logs.

- `cumaru`: massive golden-brown tropical hardwood trunk, strong buttress roots, broad dense crown, paired leaflets, restrained dark tonka pods.
- `ipe`: tall olive-brown dense trunk, spreading tropical crown, small yellow trumpet flowers, compact leaf clusters.
- `quebracho`: heavy rust-red rugged trunk, powerful low branches, dry-forest crown, sparse small leaves, deep red exposed wood detail.
- `african_blackwood`: twisted near-black multi-stem trunk, dry thorn-forest silhouette, small rounded leaves, restrained white flowers and dark seed pods.
- `lignum_vitae`: short extremely dense gray-green twisted trunk, rounded compact crown, paired dark leaves, blue-violet flowers and small orange fruit.

## Stumps

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing stumps as style references; one freshly cut stump; visible oval cut face and rings; short trunk and roots; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no ground, shadow, scenery, axe, text, or UI.

- `cumaru`: golden-brown ridged bark, dense amber-brown rings, leaf and dark tonka pods.
- `ipe`: olive-brown bark, dense olive-gold rings, leaf and yellow trumpet flowers.
- `quebracho`: rugged rust-red bark, deep red heartwood with a pale sapwood rim, small dry-forest leaves.
- `african_blackwood`: twisted charcoal bark, nearly black espresso heartwood with a cream sapwood rim, tiny leaves and white flower.
- `lignum_vitae`: twisted gray-green bark, unusual olive-green heartwood, dark leaves, blue flower and orange fruit.

## Wood icons

Common prompt: use each living tree as the subject reference and existing wood icons as style references; two short logs in a compact diagonal stack; circular cut face and rings visible; polished chunky pixel art; three-quarter top-down view; readable at 96×96; transparent background; no whole tree, stump, axe, ground, shadow, frame, text, or UI.

- `cumaru`: golden-brown bark and dense amber-brown faces, paired leaflets and dark tonka pods.
- `ipe`: olive-brown bark and olive-gold faces, leaves and yellow trumpet flowers.
- `quebracho`: rugged rust-red bark, deep red faces and pale sapwood rim, small leaf sprig.
- `african_blackwood`: twisted charcoal bark, nearly black espresso faces and cream rim, tiny leaves and white flower.
- `lignum_vitae`: gray-green twisted bark and olive-green faces, dark leaves, blue flower and orange fruit.
